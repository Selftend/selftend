
- [Do the picker sheet and the inline time control work at phone width?](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/1231) — **width was never the risk**: the day grid is percentage-sized (`99.9/7 %`), so it can only shrink — measured **7 per row and 0px overflow at every width down to 300px, both locales**. ☠️ **Bulgarian is NOT the width stress case** ("септември" 80.5 vs "September" 79.3, in a 191px slot; nothing clipped anywhere), and the #1184 footer fits with ≥81px spare at 320 — the quiet `Clear` it needs is the **existing `ghost` variant**. Two real failures instead. ☠️ **Vertical**: card heights are width-invariant, and the proposed web `datetime` sheet is **521px** — it does **not** fit an iPhone SE with Safari toolbars (505 available), and ☠️ **in landscape (327 available) every variant overflows, including the picker shipping today** — with no scroll container and `justify-center`, the clipped part is the **footer**, i.e. `Done`. ☠️ **The compact 12-hour control collides with the `Switch`** below ~324px (3.5px overlap at 320, visually confirmed) because its right edge is pinned at x=250.5 while the switch moves; ⚠️ the ticket's framing was inverted — at phone widths the row is in its **stacked** branch, which is the roomy one. Three owner rulings: **(1) the supported floor is 360dp** — the collision then cannot occur, but ⚠️ **the spec must state 320 is unsupported** rather than leave it silently broken; **(2) `PickerSheet` gains a scroll container**, which fixes the SE case, landscape, *and* the already-shipped picker; **(3) the modal gutter becomes `p-3 sm:p-6`**, keeping the 340px cap — measured to lift the day target from **38.1 → 41.5 at the 360 floor**. ☠️ `max-w-[340px]` only binds at **≥388px**, so the gutter — not the cap — sizes the card on every phone; and the real tap target is **42.1 × 42.8 with 3px dead gutters** (the library insets its `<button>`), a **ceiling the 340 cap makes 44×44 unreachable at any viewport, desktop included**. Clears WCAG 2.5.8 AA; misses Apple's 44pt by ~2px, accepted. ✅ Checked rather than assumed: the calendar has **no pan/swipe gesture** (`PanResponder` exists in exactly one library file, the web time wheel), so the scroll container is safe. Amended [#1205](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/1205) (the control's `HH`/`MM` inputs render **22 × 19**, under the AA floor, and `SegmentedControl` has no `hitSlop`); spun out [#1248](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/1248) (the longest Bulgarian reminder label truncates at the newly-ruled 360dp floor).
