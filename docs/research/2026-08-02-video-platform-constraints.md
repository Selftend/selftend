# Video platform constraints for the Selftend campaign

> Research for [Research current video constraints across Play, Reddit, YouTube, and the App Store](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/613), checked 2026-08-02. Only current first-party platform documentation is used. Platform policy and upload specifications change, so recheck the linked sources immediately before final export and publication.

## Executive conclusion

The campaign can use one editorial system and shared footage, but it should not use one literal video file everywhere.

- Google Play can use a YouTube-hosted 16:9 promotional cut, but the meaningful story must land within the first 30 seconds, the app should appear within the first 10 seconds, and the cut should omit price language and an in-video call to action.
- A Reddit feed cut should be a separate, short, silent-first 4:5 export. Organic posting and paid advertising are materially different routes; a paid Selftend campaign would fall under Reddit's restricted health-and-wellness advertising policy and requires approval before launch.
- YouTube is the least restrictive home for the 16:9 trailer and 2–4 minute walkthroughs. It can carry platform links and a direct call to action, but it still needs accurate captions, a deliberate audience setting, and non-medical claims.
- An Apple App Store app preview is not the tri-platform trailer. It is an iOS-only, device-specific, 15–30 second screen-capture asset with exact encodes, no Android or alternative-marketplace references, and an explicit disclosure that an account is required.

## Google Play details

Google Play's [preview asset requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en) make the Play listing the most constrained use of the YouTube-hosted trailer:

- Use a video URL, not a playlist or channel URL, and do not append timecodes or other parameters.
- Set the video public or unlisted, keep embedding enabled, turn off video monetization, and avoid all third-party Content ID claims that could insert ads. A claimed music track can make the video ineligible even if the channel itself is not monetized.
- Design for a complete silent-first first 30 seconds. Google may autoplay only that portion, and may not autoplay at all. The listing's 1024×500 feature graphic is the non-playing cover on the regular store listing.
- Show real functionality early and keep at least 80% of the video representative of the product. Animated device frames and title cards therefore need to remain accessory rather than the dominant visual.
- Omit “Free,” “Download now,” “Install now,” “Try now,” awards, rankings, reviews, and time-sensitive launch copy from the Play variant. These are framed as highly recommended promotional-eligibility rules rather than the upload transport's hard technical validation, but following them gives the campaign the safest reusable listing asset.

The video does not replace the app-level policy work. Google classifies mental-wellness tools as health apps. Its [Health Content and Services policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16679511?hl=en) requires an accurate Health apps declaration, a public privacy policy, a store-description disclaimer that the app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent a condition, and a reminder to consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The trailer should stay inside the narrower Selftend product boundary: guided wellness and self-help tools, not outcomes or treatment.

Google's [target-audience guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9867159?hl=en-EN) says the 13–15 and 16–17 groups may be children under local law and that selecting age groups is a claim that the app was actually designed for them. Marketing assets can also influence Google's classification. The campaign's already chosen age-neutral visual direction is compatible with that rule, but neither the video nor its metadata should state “13+” until the separate Play/app readiness work confirms the declarations and product behavior. The [metadata policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842?hl=en) independently requires listing images and video to be truthful, relevant, and suitable for a general audience.

## Reddit details

### Organic route

Reddit's current first-party help material confirms that communities individually enable or disable video posts, titles/bodies, links, domains, and other post types. Native video posts are not available in private or mature communities ([community settings](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/15484546290068-Community-settings)). Reddit does not expose a complete current general-post codec, duration, size, caption, or thumbnail specification in its public help center. The final organic export therefore needs a no-publication preflight in the exact community composer; the paid-ad limits should be treated as a conservative production envelope, not proof of organic acceptance.

Sitewide rules do not make promotion automatically forbidden. Reddit's [spam guidance](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043504051-Spam) says promotion can be legitimate, while repeated, unsolicited, irrelevant, or mostly self-interested posting can be spam; community moderators make the local decision. The post should be explicit that it comes from Selftend's maker/contributor, provide useful context beyond a bare download link, and follow each target community's rules. The current [Reddit User Agreement](https://redditinc.com/policies/user-agreement) allows use from age 13 or the higher local minimum, with guardian agreement where applicable, but that fact is not permission to target vulnerable teens or mental-health support communities with product promotion.

### Paid route

Reddit's current [Video Ads page](https://www.business.reddit.com/advertise/ad-types/video-ads) specifies MP4/MOV, at most 1 GB, ratios 1:1/4:5/16:9, and at most 15 minutes, with 60 seconds or less recommended. Ads autoplay in feeds. Reddit's current [ad-format explanation](https://www.business.reddit.com/learning-hub/articles/what-are-reddit-ads-formats-smbs) says sound is off by default, and its [2026 creative guidance](https://www.business.reddit.com/copy-creative-best-practices) reports the strongest conversion impact for sub-6-second assets, followed by 6–10 and 10–15 seconds. It also favors 4:5, visible branding in the first or final 3 seconds, and overlay text/closed captions.

For Selftend, the policy gate matters more than the generous transport limits. Reddit's current [Healthcare products and services policy](https://business.reddithelp.com/s/article/healthcare-products-and-services-policy) treats health and wellness as a restricted advertising category: advertisers and ads are vetted/preapproved, applicable laws and licensing still apply, supporting documentation can be requested, and some campaigns require direct management by Reddit Sales. A promoted organic post is still an ad: Reddit says eligible promoted posts must be public, SFW, and reviewed under Ads policy ([Promote your post](https://support.reddithelp.com/hc/en-us/articles/16750646696212-Promote-your-post)).

No paid campaign is assumed by this research. Buying or instrumenting ads would also require Selftend's explicit privacy/product review because the repository guardrails call out advertising, targeting, tracking, and behavioral nudges.

## YouTube details

YouTube is suitable for both the promotional trailer and the full walkthrough series:

- [Recommended encoding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1722171) uses MP4, H.264 progressive scan, AAC-LC audio at 48 kHz, the recorded frame rate, and BT.709 for SDR. The standard desktop ratio is 16:9, with 1920×1080 a supported 1080p delivery; the player adapts other shapes, so padding and black bars should not be baked in ([resolution and aspect ratios](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6375112)).
- The maximum is 15 minutes for an unverified channel. A verified channel can upload up to 12 hours or 256 GB, whichever is less ([upload limits](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/71673)).
- Upload a reviewed, timed UTF-8 SRT. YouTube supports SRT and other caption formats, while automatic captions can be delayed or inaccurate ([caption files](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734698), [adding captions](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2734796)).
- The upload flow allows a 100-character title, 5,000-character description, language, caption certification, audience selection, links, cards/end screens, and monetization control ([upload settings](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407)). Custom thumbnails require channel verification; current guidance recommends 3840×2160 at 16:9, with a minimum width of 640 ([thumbnail guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72431)).
- The promotional video can be selected as the non-subscriber channel trailer. YouTube says channel trailers do not show ads by default unless the video contains third-party claimed content, and each viewer stops seeing the trailer after watching it ([channel layout](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3219384)). The Play requirement is stricter, so monetization and copyright claims still need an explicit check.

A video meant for teenagers 13+ and adults can be general-audience content and set to “not made for kids”; YouTube explicitly distinguishes teen-or-older general-audience content from content directed to children under 13 ([audience guidance](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9528076)). Safe/family-friendly presentation alone does not make it “made for kids.” Scripts, descriptions, thumbnails, comments, and destination links remain subject to YouTube's [medical misinformation](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13813322), [self-harm](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2802245), and [external-links](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9054257) policies. Selftend's own no-diagnosis/no-outcomes boundary is stricter and should govern.

## Apple App Store details

Apple's [app preview guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-previews/) and [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) make an App Store product-page preview a distinct iOS asset:

- Previews may only use screen captures of the app itself. Narration and video/text overlays are allowed, including touch hotspots, but transitions must not imply functionality. Do not film a person or fingers using the device.
- Keep the preview focused on the Apple-platform experience. Guideline 2.3.10 prohibits names, icons, or imagery of other mobile platforms or alternative app marketplaces in metadata. The Android/iOS/web availability card belongs in the external YouTube/Reddit version, not in the App Store preview.
- All preview content must be suitable for a 4+ audience even when the app rating is higher. Use fictional data; Apple explicitly requires fictional rather than real account information in metadata assets.
- Because previews autoplay muted, all essential meaning needs legible on-screen copy. Apple permits an end-frame disclosure and specifically requires disclosure when login is required, so include “Account required.” Do not include a specific price.
- The current [technical specification](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications) requires 15–30 seconds, at most 500 MB, at most 30 fps, and the device-specific sizes and codecs summarized in the matrix. The current highest-resolution iPhone families accept 886×1920 portrait or 1920×886 landscape. Apple allows up to three previews per supported device size and language ([upload help](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/upload-app-previews-and-screenshots)).

Apple's numeric rating is app metadata, not promotional copy. The current rating system can display 13+ on Apple OS version 26 and newer, while earlier systems use older bands such as 12+, and regional variants also apply ([age-rating values](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/)). If Selftend's terms set a higher minimum than Apple's calculated rating, Apple instructs the developer to override upward in App Store Connect ([setting an age rating](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/set-an-app-age-rating/)). That reinforces the existing decision to withhold an explicit “13+” video claim until the age/readiness audits are resolved.

## Production envelope implied by the evidence

The most reusable source package is a modular 30 fps timeline with replaceable app-capture segments, separate narration/music/callout tracks, safe layouts for 16:9 and 4:5, and a dedicated portrait iOS sequence. A practical export family is:

1. **Google Play store-safe cut:** 1920×1080 H.264/AAC, approximately 30 seconds, app shown by 0:10, silent-first, no price statement, no CTA, no third-party claim, public or unlisted and embeddable on YouTube.
2. **YouTube channel trailer:** the same 16:9 editorial core with a YouTube-appropriate closing CTA and live Android/iOS/web links in the description; 30–45 seconds remains technically valid.
3. **Reddit feed cut:** 1080×1350 MP4/MOV, 4:5, 30 fps, preferably 6–15 seconds and no more than 60, with open captions and early/final branding. Use organically only after community-rule and composer preflight; use as an ad only after health-category and project approval.
4. **Apple App Store preview, if brought into campaign scope:** iOS-only 886×1920 H.264 at 10–12 Mbps, maximum 30 fps, 15–30 seconds, open captions, “Account required,” and no cross-platform card.
5. **YouTube walkthroughs:** 1920×1080 H.264/AAC, 2–4 minutes, reviewed SRT, custom thumbnail, and platform links in the description. These do not need store-specific derivatives unless a later campaign adds them.

Every video should retain an English transcript and timed SRT. Silent-feed exports should also carry essential designed copy or open captions, but the SRT remains necessary on YouTube for accessible, user-controllable captions. Music and fonts need a recorded license that covers worldwide, perpetual app-store and social promotion and avoids Content ID claims; the simplest safe path is original or explicitly claim-free material.

## Decisions surfaced for the campaign map

- **Reddit distribution route:** Is the “Reddit intro” an organic, transparently self-promotional community post, a paid video ad/promoted post, or an organic-first asset with paid promotion deliberately deferred? Paid introduces health-category approval, budget, targeting, measurement, and Selftend privacy-review work that organic posting does not.
- **Apple product-page scope:** Does “promote iOS too” mean only mention the live iOS listing in external trailer variants, or does this campaign also produce and submit a dedicated App Store app preview? The latter is a new deliverable, not another export of the tri-platform trailer.
- **Play versus YouTube ending:** Either create separate ending variants, or let the Play-safe video omit the spoken/on-screen CTA and put the YouTube CTA in the description/end-screen layer. A single upload with “Free” and “Download Selftend” conflicts with Google's Play preview recommendations.
- **App Store orientation and coverage:** If an Apple preview is in scope, select portrait versus landscape and whether iPhone-only coverage is sufficient. Portrait 886×1920 best matches Selftend's native phone experience; iPad previews are optional and would require their own device-specific assets.
