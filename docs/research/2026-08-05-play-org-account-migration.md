# Google Play Organization-Account Migration Research (Selftend)

All sources checked 2026-08-05 unless noted. Primary sources only (Google Play Console Help, dnb.com, Bulgarian registry portals). Where an official page would not load, that is noted explicitly.

## Conclusion (up front)

Google Play's organization-account mandate covers "Health apps, such as Medical apps and Human Subjects Research apps" — it does **not** name Health & Fitness / wellness apps, which is the category Selftend (CBT-based self-help, stress/mental-wellness) falls under in the Health apps declaration form. As of 2026-08-05 there is **no published deadline** forcing existing individual accounts with health-adjacent apps to convert; the requirement rolled out to *new* developer accounts first (announced 2024-07-17) and the official Policy Deadlines table contains no health/organization-account entry. So migration is most likely **not required** for Selftend today — the main residual risk is how the app is categorized in the Health apps declaration (declaring a "Medical > mental health" feature rather than "Health and Fitness > stress management/mental wellness" could trigger the org requirement). If migration is ever needed: any registered Bulgarian entity (ET, EOOD, foundation/association) with a D-U-N-S number (free, up to 30 days; Bulgaria is served by D&B Worldwide Network partner CRIF/ICAP CRIF) can open an org account for another one-time $25 fee, and the official app-transfer process moves users, stats, ratings, reviews, signing keys and the store listing with no downtime for a free app — but **not** testing tracks/testers.

## (a) What qualifies as an "Organization" account; D-U-N-S; Bulgarian entity forms

**Account types.** Google offers two types: personal ("students, hobbyist, or amateur developer") and organization — "choose an organization account if you're creating an account for an organization or business", i.e. "you're engaged in commercial, industrial, professional, or governmental activities". Google does **not** enumerate accepted legal-entity forms (no LLC/sole-trader/non-profit list).
Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885 (checked 2026-08-05)

**Categories that MUST use an organization account** (same page, exact wording):
- "Financial products and services, including but not limited to banking, loans, stock trading, investment funds, cryptocurrency software wallets, and cryptocurrency exchanges."
- "Health apps, such as Medical apps and Human Subjects Research apps."
- "Apps approved to use the VpnService class."
- "Government apps, including apps developed by or on behalf of a government agency."
Source: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885 (checked 2026-08-05); mirrored in Play Console Requirements: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10788890 (checked 2026-08-05)

**D-U-N-S is mandatory for org accounts.** "To create a developer account for an organization, you must have a D-U-N-S number. This is a mandatory requirement… used by Google Play to verify your identity." Organizations "can use Dun & Bradstreet to see if they already have a D-U-N-S number, or request one for free"; the process "can take up to 30 days". Regions D&B does not support can apply to Google support for an alternative verification path; known government organizations are also exempt.
Sources: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312 and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920 (both checked 2026-08-05)

**Bulgarian entity forms.** Because Google specifies no legal-form list, any entity that (1) is registered (can show "proof of business registration that confirms the current existence of the organization") and (2) can obtain a D-U-N-S number should qualify. On that basis:
- **ET (едноличен търговец / sole trader)** — a registered trader in the Bulgarian Commercial Register; D&B issues D-U-N-S to sole proprietorships, so this should qualify as an "organization" (assessment, not an explicit Google statement — see Open questions).
- **Foundation/association (фондация/сдружение)** — registered non-profit legal entity (Register of Non-Profit Legal Entities at the Registry Agency); qualifies.
- **EOOD (single-member Ltd)** — registered company; clearly qualifies. This is the cleanest fit for "organization or business".

**D-U-N-S acquisition path for Bulgaria.**
- Global request: D&B's "Get a D-U-N-S" page offers U.S., Canada and "International-based business" application paths; "There is no cost to request and receive a D-U-N-S Number." Standard processing up to 30 business days; "With the purchase of an expedited D-U-N-S Number you can get your D-U-N-S Number within eight business days" (fee not stated on page). https://www.dnb.com/en-us/smb/duns/get-a-duns.html (checked 2026-08-05); lookup of existing numbers: https://www.dnb.com/en-us/smb/duns/duns-lookup.html (checked 2026-08-05)
- Local partner: Bulgaria is covered by D&B Worldwide Network partner **CRIF** (regional listing includes Bulgaria) — operating locally as **ICAP CRIF** (Sofia office: 51B Bulgaria Blvd, fl.5, office 14, +359 2 8014 100, icapcrifbulgaria@icapcrif.com), which markets "Dun & Bradstreet Finance & Compliance Solutions" for Bulgaria/Greece/Romania/Cyprus.
Sources: https://www.dnb.com/en-us/why-dnb/partnerships/worldwide-network/partners.html ; https://www.icapcrif.com/bg/en/ ; https://icapcrif.com/en/our-services/business-information-credit-risk-services/dun-bradstreet-finance-compliance-solutions/ (all checked 2026-08-05)

## (b) Costs and verification requirements

- **Registration fee:** "There is a US$25 one-time registration fee" — per account registration, so a new organization account pays it again. "If this information is determined to be invalid, your registration fee will not be refunded." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435 (checked 2026-08-05). The app-transfer help page notes that if you create a new target account the $25 fee applies, and it **may be refundable if you close the original account after the transfer** (see (c)).
- **D-U-N-S:** free; paid expedite available (≈8 business days; fee amount not published on the page). https://www.dnb.com/en-us/smb/duns/get-a-duns.html (checked 2026-08-05)
- **Org account information required:** developer name; D-U-N-S number; organization name + address (must come from the linked Google Payments profile); organization phone number and **organization website URL** (required field, not shown on Google Play); contact name/email/phone (internal); developer email + developer phone (both shown publicly on Google Play). "All contact information must be verified using a one-time password and remain operational for the duration of your developer account." https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312 (checked 2026-08-05)
- **Identity verification documents (org):** D-U-N-S number, an official government identity document (for the person), and an "Organization document" — "proof of business registration that confirms the current existence of the organization". "All submitted documents must exactly match the information in your payment profile." Enhanced website-verification requirements apply to organization accounts created after February 2024 (Google publishes a downloadable org-verification guide). https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920 and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15574489 (both checked 2026-08-05)
- **Verification windows:** developers "can start account verification in Play Console 60 days before their chosen deadline"; failing to complete verification by the deadline leads to developer profile and apps being "removed from Google Play". https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14177239 (checked 2026-08-05)
- Phone numbers must be E.164 format, verified by SMS/voice 6-digit code; emails by 6-digit code. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920 (checked 2026-08-05)

## (c) App-transfer mechanics (individual → organization account)

Official page: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247 (checked 2026-08-05)

- **Process:** request-based (Play Console Help transfer form/checklist). Both original and target accounts must be "registered and active" and comply with all policies. You must supply the **target account's registration transaction ID** (found via the account owner's email "developer registration fee" receipt or Google Payments > Activity; strip the "0.G." / pre-"token" prefix when submitting).
- **Fees:** creating a new target account costs the US$25 registration fee again; per the transfer page this may be **refundable if you close the original account after the transfer**. Keep the old account open until the transfer completes (both accounts must be active during the transfer).
- **Payments profile:** target account needs an active payments profile only if transferring **paid apps or in-app products** — not applicable to a free app like Selftend.
- **What transfers:** "All users, statistics, data, comments, ratings, subscriptions" plus store listing information and content ratings. For Play App Signing apps, **both the upload key and app signing key transfer** (you can request new keys if concerned). Package name is unchanged (transfers move the app as-is; package names are permanent).
- **What does NOT transfer:** "Bulk export reports, estimated sales reports, and earnings reports won't be transferred"; "Test groups (open, closed, internal test, and internal sharing) can't be transferred" — testers/tracks must be rebuilt on the new account; promotions don't transfer (already-issued promo codes stay valid); Firebase / Google Analytics / AdMob linkages must be manually reconfigured.
- **Timing / downtime:** "Our support team reviews and replies to transfer requests within 2 business days." No downtime is described for free apps; the only unpublish case mentioned is temporary unpublishing for paid-app currency conversion — not applicable to a free app.
- **Gotchas:** both accounts in good standing (a terminated account cannot transfer); transaction-ID formatting; test tracks lost (Selftend's closed/open testing history would need re-setup); third-party service links re-wired manually.

## (d) The underlying driver: does the Health policy force an org account for Selftend?

- **Health Content and Services policy** (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16679511, checked 2026-08-05): requires the Health apps declaration form and a privacy policy for any app with health features; it does **not** itself impose an organization-account requirement.
- **Health apps declaration categories** (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14738291, checked 2026-08-05): "Health and Fitness" (activity tracking, nutrition/weight, period tracking, sleep, **stress management / mental wellness**) vs "Medical" (disease management, clinical decision support, **mental health**, medication management, …) vs Medical Device vs Human Subjects Research. Since 2024-08-31 "all apps will be required to have completed an accurate Health apps declaration". Selftend's honest fit is **Health and Fitness > stress management / mental wellness** (it is self-help, not diagnosis/treatment).
- **The org-account mandate** lives in the account-type docs, not the health policy: "Health apps, such as Medical apps and Human Subjects Research apps" must use an organization account (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885, checked 2026-08-05). Health & Fitness / wellness apps are **not listed**.
- **Rollout/enforcement:** announced 2024-07-17: "We're requiring developers providing the following services to register as an Organization: financial products and services, health, VPN, and government." … "This update will roll out to new developer accounts first. Existing developers will receive more information later this year." (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14993590, checked 2026-08-05). As of 2026-08-05, the official **Policy Deadlines table contains no deadline** for health apps / org-account conversion / account verification tied to this mandate (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/table/12921780, checked 2026-08-05), and no 2025–2026 policy announcement reviewed (2025-03-05, 2025-10-30, 2026-07-15) adds one.
- **Category definitions** (https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13996367, checked 2026-08-05): Health & Fitness apps "help users manage their health and fitness… track or sync information about their personal health and fitness… in areas such as fitness, nutrition, wellness, and sleep"; Medical apps "provide medical information, resources, or tools… to enhance medical care, facilitate diagnosis and treatment"; Human Subjects Research apps are IRB/EC-approved research apps. Selftend matches the first definition.

**Bottom line for Selftend:** a wellness/self-help CBT app declared under Health and Fitness does not fall under the published org-account mandate. Risk to watch: Google Play Developer Community threads exist where apps were flagged as requiring an org account based on declaration answers (e.g. thread 398183169, "App incorrectly requires Organization account although it has no medical features" — community content, not policy; noted only as a signal that declaration category choice drives enforcement).

## Open questions / assumptions

1. **Does an ET (sole trader) satisfy "organization"?** Google never enumerates legal forms; an ET is a registered trader with a Commercial Register entry and can hold a D-U-N-S number, so it should pass document checks — but this is an assessment, not a documented Google position. An EOOD or foundation is unambiguous.
2. **Will existing individual accounts with health-declared apps be forced to convert?** The 2024-07-17 announcement promised "more information" for existing developers; as of 2026-08-05 no deadline appears in the Policy Deadlines table. Assumption: no forced migration yet for existing accounts; watch Play Console notices and https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/announcements/13412212.
3. **Which declaration category does Google consider Selftend to be?** "Medical > mental health" vs "Health and Fitness > stress management / mental wellness" is the pivot: the former is in the org-mandate examples, the latter is not. Selftend's guardrails (wellness/self-help, "not a diagnosis engine and not a therapist replacement") support the Health & Fitness classification, but Google's classifier may disagree — community threads show org-account prompts triggered by declaration answers.
4. **Expedited D-U-N-S fee amount** — not published on the D&B page; would need a quote from D&B/ICAP CRIF.
5. **Bulgarian registry statutory ruling deadline** — not verified against an official source (see table); the Commercial Register Act's next-business-day/short-ruling rule is widely cited but was not confirmed from a loading official page. Marked as assumption.
6. **$25 refund on closing the old account after transfer** — the transfer help page (as retrieved) indicates the new-account fee "may be refundable if you close the original account afterward"; treat as conditional, confirm in the transfer form flow before relying on it.
7. **Website/domain verification specifics for org accounts created after Feb 2024** — Google references enhanced website verification and a downloadable org-verification guide (answer/15574489); exact domain-proof mechanics (e.g. DNS/Search Console) were not extracted from the guide itself.
