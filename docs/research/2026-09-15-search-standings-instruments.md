# What can read selftend.org's search standing on the free tiers, at what cost and cadence

Research for [What can read selftend.org's search standing on the free tiers, at what cost and cadence](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2422), under map [Search operations - audit health, a standings reading, and a read-only baseline for selftend.org](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2419).

**Every claim below was read from a primary source on 2026-09-15** - Search Console Help and Google Search Central, the Bing Webmaster Tools help centre and API reference, the Ahrefs help centre and pricing page. Prices and plan limits move; re-read before relying on a number. Where a doc is silent, the text says **docs silent** rather than guessing.

This document **does not decide whether or how often to read**. It feeds [The standings reading: whether, how often, with what, recorded where, and what it may trigger](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2425). It respects the plan's rule that Search Console impressions and clicks are a **standing-surface reading, never an arrival** (`docs/marketing-plan.md` § 3, [#2289](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2289)): nothing here proposes a script, pixel or file on the site. Every instrument below reads the site from outside, through an account, exactly as `docs/analytics.md` already records.

## Summary

- **Google Search Console is the only instrument that reads Google's side of the site, and it is complete on the free tier.** The Performance report gives queries, pages, countries, devices and dates with clicks, impressions, CTR and position; a **position-over-time chart for one query is readable in the UI without exporting** (click the query row, keep only the Average position metric). Data is 16 months deep and usually 2-3 days behind. Its emails are **default-on**: Google mails the owner on new indexing issues, on fix validation, on manual actions, on security issues and on ownership changes - so the "monitoring routine" for coverage can be _let Google email the owner_. A **monthly performance-summary email is not documented anywhere**; only milestone emails (first 50 clicks in 28 days) are. The Search Analytics API and the Looker Studio connector are free, OAuth-only, and need nothing on the site.
- **The Page indexing report's cadence is undocumented.** No Google page states how often it refreshes or whether a 10-day-old "last updated" is normal; the "2-3 days" figure belongs to the Performance report. Per-URL freshness comes from URL Inspection (last-crawl data, or a live test).
- **Bing Webmaster Tools is free, imports the Search Console property with its verification, and needs a Microsoft, Google or Facebook sign-in.** Its Search Performance report has keywords and pages with clicks, impressions, CTR and position; countries and devices as dimensions are docs silent. Emails are **opt-in** (off until "Receive Communication" is enabled), covering crawl errors, index issues, account and promotional - no performance digest. It exposes what Search Console does not: a backlink profile with a two-site comparison, a keyword-research tool, fortnightly automatic SEO reports, an on-demand Site Scan, IndexNow, and (public preview) an AI Performance report for Copilot citations. Whether a DNS-verified GSC _domain_ property appears in the import list is docs silent - the live dialog on [#2297](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2297) step 6 answers it.
- **Ahrefs Webmaster Tools - now named "Ahrefs Free" - keeps, for the verified site, Site Explorer's Backlinks, Referring domains, Anchors, Organic keywords, Top pages and Organic competitors (current snapshot, up to 1,000 rows, historical data 0), and Site Audit with scheduled crawls under 5,000 crawl credits per month per verified project.** It loses Rank Tracker (0 keywords), the Position history graph, date comparison, exports and unverified-site lookups. Alerts on Free are docs silent; the indirect evidence (0 general credits, alert emails consume credits, "Schedule Alerts" listed as a Lite addition) says no. The credit budget is 500 per month, spendable only inside verified projects.
- **A paid tier buys one thing none of the above can: a weekly position history per keyword that the owner chose.** Ahrefs Starter is $29/month for 50 tracked keywords, weekly; Lite is $129/month for 750, weekly - **no Ahrefs tier updates daily**. Semrush's free plan tracks 10 keywords on 1 website, daily for 60 days then weekly points, with a weekly email; its paid Pro tier is $139.95/month. Stated as fact for #2425 to price against a reason; `docs/costs.md` records the cancellation as deliberate.

## 1. Google Search Console

Domain property `selftend.org`, DNS-verified since 2026-07-28 (`docs/indexability.md` § 9). "Search Console is a free service" ([answer/9128668](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9128668), read 2026-09-15).

### 1.1 Performance report (Search results)

The help page has been split since the ticket was written: [answer/7576553](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553) is now the overview and links to sibling pages on metrics (17011364), dimensions (17011259), filtering and comparison (17011165) and discrepancies (17010575). All read 2026-09-15.

- **Dimensions.** The table tabs are "Queries / Pages / Countries / Devices / Search appearance / Dates" ([7576553](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553)). Countries "groups data by the country where the search originated"; Devices "desktop, tablet, or mobile"; Dates "groups your data by days, weeks, or months (based on the selected time granularity)" ([17011259](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17011259)).
- **Metrics.** Clicks, impressions, CTR, and "Average position: The average position of the topmost result from your site" - in the chart "the average position of the topmost result from your entire site", in the table "the average position in Search results for the specific URL or grouping dimension shown in the table row" ([7576553](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553)). The worked example on [17011364](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17011364): three URLs from one site at positions 1, 2, 3 read as position 1 aggregated by property and 2 aggregated by page.
- **Date range.** Default "the past three months" ([7576553](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553)). The only presets named in primary docs are the "24-hour view" (same page) and "Last 16 months" ([debugging-search-traffic-drops](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops)); the full preset list is **docs silent**. Granularity: "Hourly, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly", hourly only in the 24-hour view.
- **Retention.** No current help-centre page states the general limit (**docs silent** on 7576553 and its siblings). Google's own statements: "The Search Analytics API now returns 16 months of data, just like the Performance report" ([Search Central blog, 2018-06](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2018/06/new-url-inspection-tool-more-in-search)); "If you'd like to extend the 16 months, you could use the Search Analytics API or bulk data exports" ([debugging-search-traffic-drops](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/debugging-search-traffic-drops)); and, outside Search Console Help but Google-authored, "Search Console keeps data for the last 16 months" ([Analytics Help 10737381](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10737381)).
- **Freshness lag.** "Collected data is usually available in 2-3 days" ([17010575](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17010575), same wording on [96568](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/96568)). "The newest data in the Performance report is sometimes preliminary … may change in the next few hours"; preliminary data shows as a dotted line and only when a day with preliminary data is explicitly selected, "usually today's data and sometimes yesterday's" ([17011364](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17011364)). The 24-hour view "will appear with a delay of only a few hours" ([blog, 2024-12](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2024/12/recent-data-search-console)). Daily data is labelled in Pacific Time ([17010575](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17010575)). A fixed "final after N days" number is **docs silent**.
- **Row limit.** "The table can display a maximum of 1,000 rows, so some rare or long-tail rows might be omitted from the table, but included in the chart total" ([17010575](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17010575)); UI export is also capped at 1,000 rows ([blog, 2022-10](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/10/performance-data-deep-dive)). Irrelevant at selftend.org's one query row, recorded for completeness.
- **Position over time for one query, without exporting: yes.** "Click a query in the table to filter all report data to the selected query" ([17010961](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17010961)); the same page's recipe for position history is "Select the Average position metric and deselect all other metrics. Choose the time frame you want to analyze." Filtering is by any row click or "+ Add filter" ([17011165](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/17011165)). Caveat the docs attach: "Filtering your data by query or URL can affect the report totals due to data truncation and the omission of anonymized queries." Comparison mode compares "two values in any one grouping dimension" with a Difference column (same page).
- **Anonymised queries - the privacy floor matters at this scale.** "We might not track some queries that are made a very small number of times or those that contain personal or sensitive information"; "Tables in the performance reports omit rare queries to protect user privacy" ([96568](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/96568)). Anonymised queries are "those that aren't issued by more than a few dozen users over a two-to-three month period"; they are "included in chart totals, unless you filter by query"; "There is no row for anonymized queries in the report table or API" ([blog, 2022-10](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/10/performance-data-deep-dive)). "Anonymized queries often make up the single most common query for a site" ([12917174](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/12917174)). For a site at 22 impressions in 28 days, most of the impression total can sit in queries the table will never name; a _new query row appearing_ means a query crossed the anonymisation threshold, not that it first occurred.

### 1.2 Email notifications Search Console sends by itself

- **Master switch, default on.** "This checkbox specifies whether or not Search Console should email notifications of any kind to you, for all of your properties. Default is 'enabled'." Preferences are per user, not per property; unsubscribed messages remain "visible only in the Search Console message panel" ([140528](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/140528)). Path: User settings > Email preferences; "To disable specific messages, select Mute … To disable all messages, uncheck Enable email notifications" ([9130064](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9130064)). Subscriptions are managed by message ID ("Message type: [WNC-XXXXXXX]" in the email); the first opt-out per type is the email's Unsubscribe link ([140528](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/140528)). The message panel holds 2,000 messages ([9388335](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9388335)).
- **What is sent** (each documented): "Search Console sends messages to website owners when an important event occurs on their site" ([9130064](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9130064)); message types are categorised as "Coverage, Enhancement types, Performance, and others" and are "our main way to let you know when your website has a new issue" ([blog, 2019-12](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2019/12/search-console-messages)). Indexing fix validation: "You'll get an email when Google has confirmed your fix on all URLs, or conversely, if Google has found remaining instances of that issue" ([7440203](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203)). Manual actions are notified "in the Manual actions report and in the Search Console message center" ([9044175](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9044175)). Security-issue reviews: "You will be informed of progress by email" ([9044101](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9044101)). Ownership: a "New owner" email type exists, and "If a removed owner reverifies themselves, all current owners will receive a notification" ([140528](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/140528), [7687615](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7687615)). Guidelines: "you'll receive email if any unusual events occur with your website" ([6258314](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/6258314)).
- **Performance emails that exist are milestones, not a digest.** "When your site reaches 50 clicks from Google Search in 28 days for the first time, you'll receive an email celebrating the achievement" ([16543604](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16543604)); Search Console Insights sends milestone emails ([10729962](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/10729962); [blog, 2025-06](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2025/06/search-console-insights)).
- **A monthly "Search performance" summary or "Top performing" email: docs silent.** No help-centre or Search Central page describes one or its contents. The ticket's hope that Google mails a monthly figure cannot be confirmed from primary docs; what _can_ be confirmed is that **coverage and security events are emailed by default**.
- The per-type default-on list beyond the master checkbox is **docs silent**; the doc implies every type is on until unsubscribed.

### 1.3 Search Analytics API, Looker Studio connector, bulk export

- **Free.** "All use of Google Search Console API is free of charge. However, it is subject to usage limits" ([pricing](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/pricing)).
- **Quota** ([limits](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/limits)). Search Analytics: per-site 1,200 QPM; per-user 1,200 QPM; per-project 30,000,000 QPD and 40,000 QPM. URL Inspection: per-site 2,000 QPD and 600 QPM. Export cap: "50,000 rows per day per site per search type" for the API and the Looker Studio connector alike ([blog, 2022-10](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2022/10/performance-data-deep-dive)). One reading a month of a one-row site is nowhere near any of these.
- **Shape.** `rowLimit` 1-25,000 (default 1,000), `startRow` for paging; `dataState` `final` (default), `all` (includes fresh data) or `hourly_all`; dimensions include `date` and `hour`; filterable by country, device, page, query, searchAppearance ([searchanalytics/query](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/searchanalytics/query)). "The query() method exposes all the data available in the Performance report" ([how-to](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/how-tos/search_analytics)). Retention via the API is docs silent on the reference pages; the 2018-06 blog says 16 months.
- **Nothing on the site.** "Your application must use OAuth 2.0 to authorize requests. No other authorization protocols are supported"; scopes `webmasters` and `webmasters.readonly` ([authorizing](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/how-tos/authorizing)). "Your account must have the appropriate Search Console permission on a given property in order to call that method on that property" ([prereqs](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/prereqs)). Neither page mentions a tag, script or file; access is account-permission based. How a service account is granted that permission is docs silent on those pages.
- **Looker Studio connector** (Google doc outside Search Console Help: [connect-to-search-console](https://docs.cloud.google.com/looker/docs/studio/connect-to-search-console), where the old support URL redirects). Requires being "signed in to Data Studio and Search Console with the same Google Account" with "at least view permission on the property"; two aggregation methods, Site Impression and URL Impression; "To protect user privacy, Search Analytics doesn't show all data". Nothing on the site. Connector pricing is docs silent on that page beyond "Google Search Console is a free service".
- **Bulk data export to BigQuery** ([12917675](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/12917675)). No Search Console-side charge is described; "Data is subject to Google Cloud storage and query costs, but there is a free usage level". Daily: "Search Console will export each day's data on a daily basis until you stop the export" ([12919198](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/12919198)); first export "up to 48 hours" after setup; no backfill ("use the Search Console API"). Retention is whatever partition expiration is set ("14 days or longer"). It exposes anonymised rows as a flag, still without the text: `is_anonymized_query` with a null query ([12917991](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/12917991)). Needs a Google Cloud project; disproportionate to one row a month, recorded because it is the only path past the 16-month horizon.

### 1.4 Page indexing report cadence

- [7440203](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203) states **no update cadence and no lag figure - docs silent**. It says only: the example-URL list "is limited to 1,000 items"; "When you add new content, it can take a few days for Google to index it"; "If the page is not in the Page indexing report but it is listed as indexed in the URL Inspection report, it was probably indexed recently, and will appear in the Page indexing report soon"; validation "can take several days or even longer".
- Whether a "last updated" 10 days in the past is normal is **not addressed by any primary doc found**. The nearest general statements: "We're always working to increase the update frequency for your site data" and "You can see last updated dates on most reports" ([96568](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/96568)). The "2-3 days" figure belongs to the Performance report's discrepancy page, not to indexing.
- **Fresher per-URL data.** URL Inspection "is not a live test. The results shown are from most recently indexed version of a page"; it shows "Last crawl" and the Google-selected canonical, which "can be a few hours out of date from our index value" ([9012289](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9012289)); a live test exists but "doesn't test all the issues covered by the Page indexing report" ([7440203](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7440203)). The Sitemaps report shows "Last read" (the last fetch) and "Discovered pages", with a link to the indexing of that sitemap's URLs ([7451001](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7451001)) - a fetch date, not an index state.
- Implication for #2402's coverage read: eight-of-eight can be confirmed per URL through URL Inspection on the day, while the report's own summary may trail by an undocumented interval.

## 2. Bing Webmaster Tools

The help pages at `bing.com/webmasters/help/*` are client-rendered; a plain fetch returns only the title. They were read by rendering in a browser and pulling the article body from the help content endpoint, and quotes are verbatim from those pages. A future re-check needs a browser.

- **Sign-in and cost.** "You can sign up using an existing Microsoft, Google or Facebook account. Alternatively, you can create a new Microsoft account" ([getting-started checklist](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/getting-started-checklist-66a806de)); the same page calls it "a free Bing Webmaster Tools account". The owner's Google account therefore suffices; a sign-in is required for every reading, and the last attempt on #2297 found the session signed out.
- **Import from Search Console** ([add-and-verify-site](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/add-and-verify-site-12184f8b)). "If you have a verified Google Search Console account, you can import the settings (user details, level, etc.) along with the property directly into Bing"; Bing "needs permission to access information from your Search Console account", lists the verified sites "along with the Sitemaps", and "On successful completion, the selected sites will be added and get automatically verified in Bing Webmaster Tools" - **no DNS or meta change**. Verification is then _dependent_ on the link: "Bing Webmaster Tools will periodically validate the site ownership status by accessing your Search Console account", any new sitemap is imported automatically, and disconnecting means "you will need to reverify the previously imported sites" ([refreshed-webmaster-tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/refreshed-webmaster-tools-7c7d2533)). "Once your site is added, it usually takes 48 hours for our system to generate analytics and data." **Whether a DNS-verified GSC domain property (`sc-domain:selftend.org`) appears in the import list is docs silent** - the page says only "the list of verified sites" and that "Some of the site URLs may have been normalized and grouped together". The live import dialog on #2297 step 6 settles it.
- **Search Performance** ([search-performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/search-performance-c680da36)). Metrics: Impressions, Clicks, average CTR, "Average Position - … available only for Web traffic source", plus crawl requests, crawl errors and indexed pages. Dimensions: **Keywords** and **Pages**, and a traffic-source filter (All; Web and Chat; News; Images; Videos; Knowledge Panel; Crawl and Indexing) - "Keywords and Pages data is only available for Web traffic". **Countries and devices as dimensions: docs silent.** Clicking a keyword shows the pages served for it, and clicking a page gives "position-wise performance metrics for selected keyword and page combination" - a table by rank position, **not a position-over-time chart; that chart in the UI is docs silent**. It exists through the API (`GetQueryPageDetailStats`, per-day `Position`, "updated every week"). Retention **contradicts across Microsoft's own pages**: the help page says "(up to) the past six months"; the blog says "Bing Webmaster Tools now offers 16 months of historical data in the Search Performance feature" ([blog, 2024-10](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/October-2024/Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Extends-Search-Performance-Data-to-16-Months), reaffirmed [blog, 2025-06](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/June-2025/Start-Using-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-to-Improve-Your-Site-Visibility)). No backfill: "data collection starts from the time you first add your website … you cannot see backdated data" - **every month the import waits is a month Bing never reports**. Ongoing lag in days: **docs silent**; the API reference says daily updates for site-level clicks/impressions and weekly for per-query and per-page series. Export to CSV; period-over-period comparison ([blog, 2025-03](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/March-2025/Supercharge-Your-Search-Performance-with-Bing-Webmaster-Tools)).
- **Index reports.** Site Explorer shows folder-level URL counts, clicks, impressions, backlinks, and crawl buckets "Indexed / Error / Warning / Excluded", and per URL the last-crawled and discovered dates, HTTP code and robots test ([site-explorer](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/site-explorer-c680da37)); update cadence docs silent. URL Inspection gives index, SEO and markup cards, and a "Live URL" fetch that "allows you to see exactly what the Bingbot sees" ([url-inspection](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/url-inspection-55a30305)). The dashboard's reports "are generated only after 48 hours" of data ([refreshed-webmaster-tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/refreshed-webmaster-tools-7c7d2533)).
- **Email alerting - opt-in.** "In order to receive alerts via email, you must give permission to Bing Webmaster Tools to email you by enabling the Receive Communication field. You can also select the frequency of emails you would like to receive, and the issue types" ([refreshed-webmaster-tools](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/refreshed-webmaster-tools-7c7d2533)); configured under the gear icon > Communication preference ([email-alert-preferences](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/how-to-set-email-alert-preferences-c73a478c)). Emails are digests of the in-app Notifications Center; selectable types "Crawl errors", "Index issues", "Account related", "Promotional"; Microsoft recommends "daily digests" ([notifications-center](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/using-the-notifications-center-2d8ad7ca)). Crawl-error alerts fire on "a significant or significantly increasing number of issues" - 401, 403, 5xx, DNS failures, crawl-delay in robots.txt ([crawl-error-alerts](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/crawl-error-alerts-e29a3f3e)). An explicit "default = off" sentence and the frequency list are docs silent; a performance-summary email is docs silent. Site Scan has a per-scan email of its own.
- **What it exposes that Search Console does not** (each from Microsoft's docs): **Backlinks** with "aggregated count of referring pages, referring domains and anchor texts detected by Bing", a comparison "with up to two other sites' backlinks" and a "Show domains not linking to my site" filter ([backlinks](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/backlinks-c3a334e8)) - Microsoft's own comparison names this as a Search Console gap ([blog, 2024-08](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/August-2024/Bing-Webmaster-Tools-or-Google-Search-Console-A-Comparison)); **Keyword Research** with volume, trend, country/language/device filters and the top-10 URLs per root keyword ([keyword-research](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/keyword-research-628070b6)) - a demand read that partially replaces what Ahrefs Keywords Explorer loses on 2026-10-10; **SEO Reports** that "run automatically on any domain, which you have verified in your account, every alternate week" (checklist page); **Site Scan**, "an on-demand site audit tool" with a monthly page quota ([site-scan](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/site-scan-623520c9)); **IndexNow** and its insights ([indexnow](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/indexnow-0z209wby)); chat and Copilot traffic in the same report, plus an **AI Performance** report in public preview ([ai-performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c)). Microsoft states reach, not share: Bing "powers … Yahoo, DuckDuckGo, Microsoft Start, and AI tools like ChatGPT" ([blog, 2025-06](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/June-2025/Start-Using-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-to-Improve-Your-Site-Visibility)); whether those clicks are counted in Search Performance is docs silent. The portal also links Microsoft Clarity, "a free-to-use analytics product" - an on-site script, refused here by the privacy policy.
- **API.** Free (no pricing page; the key is issued inside the free portal): one API key per user under Settings > API Access, valid for all verified sites, or OAuth 2.0 with `webmaster.read` ([getting-access](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/bingwebmaster/getting-access), [oauth2](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/bingwebmaster/oauth2)). Read-API rate limits are not numerically documented ("Quotas, rate limits, and permissions are unchanged" - [SOAP/POX retirement](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/soap-pox-api-deprecation-s0appox01)); only submission quotas are numbered. **SOAP and POX were retired 2026-08-31**; anything built now uses the JSON endpoint ([api-protocols](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/bingwebmaster/api-protocols)). Every read method takes only `siteUrl` plus the key ([IWebmasterApi](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/dotnet/api/microsoft.bing.webmaster.api.interfaces.iwebmasterapi?view=bing-webmaster-dotnet)); nothing on the site.

## 3. Ahrefs Webmaster Tools, from 2026-10-10

"Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is now Ahrefs Free" - "the same plan, renamed" ([ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools](https://ahrefs.com/webmaster-tools), [ahrefs.com/free](https://ahrefs.com/free), read 2026-09-15). The help centre calls it the "Ahrefs Free account"; article dates show only "Updated over a week ago".

- **Site Explorer, verified site** ([what-can-i-use-for-free](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/13002606-what-can-i-use-for-free-in-ahrefs)): Overview; Backlinks, Referring domains, Anchors; Organic keywords, Organic positions, Top pages, Organic competitors; Paid keywords, Ads, Paid pages; Best by links, Crawled pages; Internal links, Most linked pages, Internal anchors - "Available for websites you can prove ownership for". "1000 backlinks & keywords visible at once". **Historical data 0** on Free (Starter 1 month, Lite 6 months) ([about-ahrefs-starter-plan](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9419051-about-ahrefs-starter-plan); [difference-between-all-plans](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/6117209-what-s-the-difference-between-all-ahrefs-plans)). "Export rows per month None". Unverified projects 0. ⚠️ The Starter article's table reads "Max rows per report -" and "Max rows in Top pages report 0" for Free, which conflicts with the 1,000 figure on the AWT page and in 13002606; recorded as seen, unresolved.
- **Organic keywords on Free is a current snapshot, not a history.** The "Position History graph" is listed as something Starter adds over Free ([6117209](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/6117209-what-s-the-difference-between-all-ahrefs-plans)); "How far back you can view ranking history for a keyword depends on your subscription tier" ([580856](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/580856-can-i-see-the-ranking-history-of-a-given-keyword)) and Free's tier is 0. Opening the graph costs a credit on the plans that have it ([what-actions-consume-credits](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/6061658-what-actions-consume-credits)).
- **Rank Tracker: 0 keywords on Free** ([9419051](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9419051-about-ahrefs-starter-plan): Free 0, Starter 50, Lite 750); "Rank Tracker (not included in Ahrefs Free)" ([Ahrefs blog, 2024-09, feature announcement](https://ahrefs.com/blog/starter-plan/)). Rank Tracker history "Starts from when you first added the keyword" ([580856](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/580856-can-i-see-the-ranking-history-of-a-given-keyword)); whether stored history survives the downgrade is **docs silent** - the baseline ticket should read it before 2026-10-10.
- **Site Audit.** "For each verified project, the first 5,000 crawl credits per month spent to crawl this project is free. This is available for users on all plans" ([how-are-crawl-credits-spent](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/3119402-how-are-crawl-credits-in-site-audit-spent)); only 200-status internal HTML pages consume credits, so eight public routes cost eight. **Scheduled crawls exist and are the default**: "Schedule - This configures when to schedule site audit to crawl your website regularly, and how often it does so … toggle 'Run scheduled Crawls' off" ([site-audit-settings](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9082329-how-should-i-configure-my-site-audit-settings)); the frequencies are weekly, monthly or daily ([how-always-on-audit-works](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/10957674-how-always-on-audit-works)), and the free "Basic" project tier is listed with "Scheduled site audits" ([project-boost-add-ons](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/10130957-what-are-project-boost-add-ons)). No doc restricts scheduled-crawl frequency by plan (**docs silent** on daily for Free specifically). The monthly audit on the 9th in `docs/operations-runbook.md` therefore continues unchanged. Always-on audit (continuous crawl plus email alerts on critical issues) "On Free and Starter plans … is not available by default and can only be enabled for boosted projects" ([10957674](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/10957674-how-always-on-audit-works)); boost prices docs silent.
- **Alerts** (backlinks, new keywords, mentions, Rank Tracker, Site Audit - [914827](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/914827-how-do-i-change-the-frequency-or-disable-ahrefs-email-alerts)). **Availability on Free is docs silent**: no article says Free has or lacks them. Three indirect facts point to no: "Alert emails consume credits per month" ([4804104](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/4804104-how-can-i-set-up-ahrefs-alerts-for-my-client)) while Free has 0 general credits; "Schedule Alerts" is listed among what Lite adds over Starter ([6117209](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/6117209-what-s-the-difference-between-all-ahrefs-plans)); and neither the AWT nor the Free page lists Alerts. Whether the 500 verified-project credits can pay for alert emails is unverified. The map's charting note ("Alerts do not survive") is consistent with this but is not confirmed by a doc.
- **Credit budget.** "Credits per month - Ahrefs Free account 0 | Starter 200 | Lite 1000" plus "Additional credits that can be used on all verified projects 500 … can only be spent for URLs within any of the verified projects" ([9419051](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9419051-about-ahrefs-starter-plan); same in [14333008](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/14333008-about-rows-export-rows-and-user-limits)). Opening a report costs 1 credit; filters, date changes and SERP updates cost more; "Site Audit, Brand Radar and Rank Tracker DO NOT consume credits" ([6061658](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/6061658-what-actions-consume-credits)). So the free reading budget is **500 credits a month inside the verified project - not unlimited**; whether the listed free Site Explorer reports draw on those 500 or are exempt is docs silent (the AWT page says "500 credits total Site Explorer"). One monthly reading of Overview, Organic keywords, Top pages and Referring domains is four credits.
- **Freshness.** Organic-keyword SERPs update "anything from multiple times a day, to once in 1-2 months … lower-volume keywords might only be refreshed every few weeks"; "There is no option to initiate the update manually" ([1077603](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/1077603-how-often-is-the-data-in-keywords-explorer-and-site-explorer-updated)). Comparison: Rank Tracker "Every 7 days"; Organic keywords "Daily - several months (programmatically decided based on their search volume)" ([2445174](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/2445174-difference-between-the-rank-tracker-and-site-explorer-s-organic-keywords-report)). For a site at 0 organic keywords in Ahrefs' index, no plan changes this.
- ⚠️ Doc inconsistency: the Free-vs-Starter table in 6117209 shows values ("Power user credits 200 | 200", "Rank Tracker Keywords 50 | 750") that match the Starter-vs-Lite pair and contradict the Starter article and its own prose. The Starter article (9419051) is the reliable table.

## 4. What a paid tier adds that none of the above can

Stated as fact for #2425 to price against a reason, not as a recommendation. `docs/costs.md` records the cancellation as deliberate and says a return is a fresh subscription, never a lapsed one.

**The one capability no free instrument has: a position history per keyword the owner chose, on a fixed cadence.** Search Console gives position history only for queries that actually produced impressions above the anonymisation floor; Ahrefs Free gives current positions only; Bing gives weekly per-query positions only through the API and only for queries that produced traffic.

- **Ahrefs** ([ahrefs.com/pricing](https://ahrefs.com/pricing), USD, per month, read 2026-09-15; "We never run discounts"; non-profit pricing docs silent):

  | Starter  | $29   | 50                    | weekly | 1 month         | 200                       |
  | Lite     | $129  | 750                   | weekly | 6 months        | 1,000 per user            |
  | Standard | $249  | 2,000                 | weekly | 2 years         | unlimited                 |

  Pricing-page row verbatim: "Rank Tracker Tracked keywords 750 2,000 5,000 From 10,000 Update frequency Weekly Weekly Weekly Weekly" - **weekly on every tier; no Ahrefs plan updates daily** ("With all subscription plans, the default update frequency for the tracked keywords in Rank Tracker is weekly" - [604245](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/604245-how-frequently-do-my-tracked-keywords-get-updated)). The old "Rank Tracker Pro" daily article returns 404. Starter: "can only be renewed monthly", "Additional users and credits can't be bought on this plan", "Features and limits on this plan may be changed without prior notice" ([9419051](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/9419051-about-ahrefs-starter-plan)). Starter also restores Keywords Explorer and the Position history graph.

- **Semrush free plan** - the one reputable free rank tracker found, stated as fact ([position-tracking limits](https://www.semrush.com/kb/696-what-are-the-limits-of-my-position-tracking-campaign), [subscriptions](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1011-subscriptions), read 2026-09-15): "10 keywords across 1 website to monitor"; "The tool tracks daily results for 60 days. After 60 days, Position Tracking displays weekly data points"; "Daily updates and weekly email reports"; "1 demo project, 10 reports per day across all vital tools". Paid: Pro "$139.95/month … 500 keywords to track", Guru $249.95, Business $499.95 ([seo-toolkit-pricing-limits](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1547-seo-toolkit-pricing-limits), USD; the public pricing page served EUR to this fetch, so it is geo-dependent). It reads Google's SERP from outside; nothing on the site. Note for #2425: a keyword list the owner types into a rank tracker is a choice made _for a query_, which is the plan's definition of where pursuit begins - the tool is read-only, but the act of choosing what to track is the line § 6 draws.
