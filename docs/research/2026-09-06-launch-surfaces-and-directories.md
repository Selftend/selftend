# Launch surfaces, directories and volunteer platforms — what will actually take Selftend

**All facts checked 2026-09-06.** Primary sources linked inline. "Not published" means the site does not state it; nothing here is estimated to fill a gap, and every fetch failure is named in the last section rather than papered over.

This file is **evidence, not a decision.** It was gathered to answer three owner questions — is there a better-known alternative to PeerPush, is Product Hunt one, and should TimeHeroes be approached because Selftend is unpaid work — and it deliberately stops short of answering them. `docs/marketing-plan.md` § _Square 3 — Media_ is where the decisions live.

> ⚠️ **Read Square 3 before acting on any row here.** The plan admits channels **one at a time**, on 8-week windows, because attribution is refused and differential measurement is arithmetically dead at about one arrival a week. A list of thirty places that would accept a listing is not a list of thirty things to do.

## 1. PeerPush

<https://peerpush.com>

**What it is.** A product-discovery directory with a launch queue and a mutual-support scoring layer, positioned by its own copy against Product Hunt's single-day model: _"List your product once. People find it, AI assistants recommend it."_ ([home](https://peerpush.com)); _"Unlike traditional product directories that act as passive listings, PeerPush creates an active ecosystem where builders help each other grow."_ ([about](https://peerpush.com/about))

**☠️ Selftend's listing is not live.** <https://peerpush.com/p/selftend> shows status **"Coming soon"** — 1 upvote, 1 follower, 1 comment; categories Health & Wellness / Open Source / Mental Health; pricing "Free"; listed as an alternative to Finch, Headspace, Daylio, How We Feel. This is consistent with the free tier and is the expected state of a free submission, not a fault in the submission.

**Pricing** ([pricing](https://peerpush.com/pricing)) — the free tier is a queue, and the only published lever is money:

| Tier            | Price                    | What it buys                                                             |
| --------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Free            | $0                       | _"Wait in line behind paid users."_ **No wait length is published.**     |
| Standard Launch | $39 one-time             | _"Live in 60 seconds"_, permanent link, 10 points                        |
| —               | $89 one-time (from $129) | + 7 days featured, _"~20,000 estimated impressions"_                     |
| —               | $229 one-time (from $499)| + 30 days featured, _"~100,000 estimated impressions"_                   |
| Outrank board   | from $5                  | Bidding placement                                                        |

**Reciprocity is not mandatory but is priced into ranking.** _"Earn PeerPush Points by upvoting, commenting, and engaging with other products. Active participation helps your own products rank higher."_ Points accrue for upvoting, commenting, visiting product sites and posting updates; ranking factors are stated as Community Engagement, Votes, Age and Growth Tracking ([about](https://peerpush.com/about), [launch post](https://peerpush.com/blog/introducing-peerpush)). **This is an ongoing owner-time cost, not a one-time form-fill** — which is the property that decides whether a surface can be admitted as zero-effort.

**⚠️ Traffic claims are self-reported and internally inconsistent.**

- Homepage: **802,638 monthly visitors**, 162 countries, 9.3M reads by 32 AI systems in 30 days.
- About page: **820k+ monthly visitors**, 34k+ newsletter subscribers, 44k+ active builders, 20,574 AI discoveries/30d.
- Third-party figures diverge beyond reconciliation: 0.7M/mo and DR 64 ([blastra](https://blastra.io/directories/peerpush/)); "320k+ monthly visitors" ([noonlaunch](https://noonlaunch.com/blog/peerpush-review)); DR 73 with monthly traffic of "5" ([launchdirectories](https://launchdirectories.com/directory/peerpush)).

**No independently audited figure was found.** Two numbers on one site that disagree with each other are not a measurement.

**Health rules** ([terms](https://peerpush.com/terms)). Prohibited: astrology, tarot, numerology, horoscopes, psychic readings, homeopathy, energy healing _"and similar products that make predictive claims about a user's personal future or events, or **unsubstantiated health claims**"_; and _"Pharmaceutical products or medical devices without proper licensing and approval."_ A self-help app that makes no medical claim is not restricted, and PeerPush carries Mental Health as a category. **No individual-versus-company restriction is published.**

**Maintained:** yes — roughly ten blog posts in the 22 days before checking, most recent about six days prior ([blog](https://peerpush.com/blog)). The launch post is dated 2025-06-10, updated 2026-08-18. **Owner/founder is not published**; the Crunchbase entry is obfuscated.

`/guidelines`, `/products` and `/launches` return **404**; `/submit` renders a login screen only.

## 2. Product Hunt

<https://www.producthunt.com>

**Launching is free.** No fee appears anywhere in the posting or launch-preparation documentation ([how to post](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product), [preparing for launch](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch)). Promoted Products are explicitly _not_ available to new launches ([promotion](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/1444961-how-do-i-get-my-product-promoted)), and advertising starts at **$5,000** self-serve / **$10,000 a month** managed ([sponsor](https://www.producthunt.com/sponsor)) — irrelevant at this scale.

**Personal accounts only, which fits an individual maintainer exactly.** _"Company accounts cannot hunt or post products."_ The profile must _"appear human and represent an individual, not a company, service, brand, or organization"_ — real first and last name, own photo, non-generic username ([community guidelines](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/3615694-community-guidelines)). A maker may post their own product; a separate hunter is optional.

**Already-launched products are accepted — the guidelines require the opposite of newness.** _"**Live Products**: We only feature products that are currently available"_; waitlisted or no-access products are not featured ([featuring guidelines](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/9883485-product-hunt-featuring-guidelines)). The six-month rule governs only _re_-posting: wait six months between posts for the same product or root domain, plus a significant update — and _"New UIs, pricing plan changes, etc. are not considered significant updates"_ ([relaunching](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/484934-can-i-relaunch-my-product)). **One shot, effectively.**

**☠️ No health or mental-health restriction is published.** The featuring guidelines' exclusion list is directories, templates, boilerplates, podcasts, courses, reports, events, books, services, commerce and deal sites, waitlisted products, hardware without a digital component, and non-functional Kickstarters. Health is absent, and the community guidelines do not mention health, medical or wellness at all. Product Hunt runs a live [Mental Health category with 370 products](https://www.producthunt.com/categories/mental-health). **The objection to Product Hunt cannot be sourced to its rules or its price.**

**No non-profit or free-product policy is published.** A "Free" pricing tag is a required field, and the [Open Source topic](https://www.producthunt.com/topics/open-source) holds 2,876 products.

**What launch day costs.** Schedule up to a month ahead; posts go live 12:01 AM PST on 24-hour PST cycles. Required: product URL, name, tagline, description (260 characters per the help doc; community reports a raise to 500), topics, pricing tag, a 240×240 thumbnail, a gallery of two or more 1270×760 images, and a first comment — Product Hunt's own figure is that _"70% of products who achieved Product of the Day, Week, or Month had a first comment by the maker."_ **Soliciting upvotes is banned**: mass messaging, asking for upvotes, bots and incentives are all prohibited. In practice this is a concentrated day of the maker's own presence, in English.

**⚠️ Traffic: the platform's own figures, and nothing verified below them.** Product Hunt publishes **9M+ monthly pageviews, 200k+ monthly signed-in members, 650k+ newsletter subscribers** on its advertiser page ([sponsor](https://www.producthunt.com/sponsor)) — figures sold to advertisers, not audited. Similarweb (July 2026) puts it at global rank **#15,386**, category rank #388, traffic **down 12.98% month over month**, after earlier readings showed growth (rank 19,104 → 17,092 across Q4 2025; +12.15% MoM in January 2026; +23.67% MoM in March 2026). **Volatile, not collapsing** ([similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/website/producthunt.com/)).

**⚠️ Referral traffic for a mid-ranking launch is not published by Product Hunt.** Only marketing-blog estimates exist, none with a disclosed method:

- #1–3 → 5,000–15,000 visitors; #4–10 → 1,000–3,000; #11–30 → 300–700; #31+ → under 300. The author states no dataset or API source ([causo, updated 2026-09-01](https://hub.causo.ai/guides/product-hunt-traffic-data-2026)).
- Featured products 1,000–5,000 visitors / 10–150 signups; non-featured 100–500 visitors / 1–15 signups; and a claim that only ~10% of submissions are now **featured**, down from 60–98% in 2020–2023 — sourced to Tetriz.io / Awesome Directories (Nov 2025), not to Product Hunt. The same page states plainly: _"No verified Product Hunt performance data published by the platform directly."_ ([shno](https://www.shno.co/marketing-statistics/product-hunt-launch-statistics))

**Has its relevance changed?** Product Hunt has published **no traffic or referral commentary**. Its 2025 recap thread — posted by a community member, not staff — lists only product changes (forums replacing discussions, "Coming Soon" pages removed, comment downvotes added then removed, the Golden Kitty Awards replaced by "Orbit") and makes **no claim about traffic, featuring rate or referral value** ([recap](https://www.producthunt.com/p/general/how-has-product-hunt-changed-in-the-last-year-2025-recap)). Similarweb is the only measurement available.

## 3. TimeHeroes

<https://timeheroes.org>

**What it lists: volunteering missions (мисии), not organisations and not products.** Site counters: **3,435 missions · 103,224 "heroes" · 1,217 organisers · 415 locations** across Bulgaria. Bulgarian-first with an English toggle. A separate [organiser directory](https://timeheroes.org/bg/organizations) runs to roughly 61 pages.

**☠️ An individual may post — there is no published legal-entity requirement.** The FAQ says, translated: _"If you are an informal group or an individual with an event idea (park cleanup, for example) — you can recruit people through our site"_ ([FAQ](https://timeheroes.org/bg/pages/faq)). The About page says missions come from _"individuals or organizations that need help"_ who complete the submission form and meet the criteria ([about](https://timeheroes.org/bg/pages/aboutus)). **No Bulstat/EIK requirement is published**, so [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-store-seller-stays-an-individual.md) declining the charity entity does not close this door. ⚠️ Caveat: the visible organiser directory shows only registered NGOs and foundations — no individuals — so the published rule and the visible practice differ.

**Remote and skills-based missions are published, including translation.** Two live examples: [Превеждай актуални материали за детското развитие](https://timeheroes.org/bg/premature/prevod-angliiski-materiali-detsko-razvitie-2021) and [Превеждай статии и видеа за изграждане на характер вкъщи и в училище](https://timeheroes.org/bg/kauzi-i-nachinania/prevod-angliiski-statii-videa), plus [online promotion missions](https://timeheroes.org/bg/frgi/173). The bulk of the platform is local and event-based, and the FAQ does **not** name remote, coding or design volunteering as a category — the evidence is the published missions themselves, not a stated policy.

**☠️ Published restrictions that shape what a Selftend mission could be** ([FAQ](https://timeheroes.org/bg/pages/faq)). TimeHeroes does not publish: political or religious missions; flash-mob recruitment; money-raising campaigns (non-financial help only — time, labour, goods); requests submitted less than **7 days** before the deadline; **events needing only one or two volunteers**; or missions without _"clear identified end needs."_ It reserves the right to refuse publication and terminate missions at its discretion.

**What that means for shape, stated as a constraint rather than a plan.** The platform brokers a bounded need for volunteers' time. A publishable mission is therefore something like _help translate or test Selftend_ — a contributor ask. _Come and use Selftend_ is not a volunteering mission and has no matching mission type. This places TimeHeroes on the **project axis** (`docs/marketing-plan.md` § 5), where arrivals are fixed at zero by rule, not among the acquisition channels of Square 3.

**Free and active.** No fee is published anywhere; the platform is funded by the Active Citizens Fund and the America for Bulgaria Foundation. The homepage carried a mission with a deadline of **30 September 2026** at time of checking. **No rules specific to software projects seeking contributors or translators are published.**

`/bg/missions` and `/en/missions` return **404** to direct fetch, so the "online" facet could not be enumerated.

## 4. Other directories and launch platforms

### F-Droid — ⚑ probably closed, and not for the reason usually given

<https://f-droid.org>

The [inclusion policy](https://f-droid.org/en/docs/Inclusion_Policy/) requires FLOSS throughout and forbids _"proprietary tracking or advertising libraries and analytics tools such as Google Play Services"_ — **naming Google Play Services, Firebase and Crashlytics explicitly** — plus a 100% FLOSS build toolchain. Free, no legal entity, no health restriction, actively maintained (news 2026-08-31, 2026-07-31, 2026-04-28). Reproducible builds are a per-app status indicator, **not an inclusion precondition** ([docs](https://f-droid.org/docs/Reproducible_Builds/)).

**☠️ The binding rule is the Firebase ban, which `expo-notifications` triggers via FCM — not any policy about Expo.** A forum member states _"Expo depends on many non-free components… Apps using Expo may not be included into F-Droid"_ ([forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/t/tutorial-on-how-to-package-an-app-made-with-react-native-expo/8497)), but **that is a community statement and no F-Droid policy page names Expo.** One user got an Expo APK building only after stripping `com.google.android.gms`, `com.facebook.android`, Segment, Branch and Crashlytics ([forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/t/expo-and-react-native-apps/701)); there is no evidence it was ever merged, and **no Managed-Expo or EAS-built app was found in the main F-Droid repo.**

React Native alone is possible but unsupported: F-Droid's own [2020 how-to](https://f-droid.org/2020/10/14/adding-react-native-app-to-f-droid.html) runs `yarn install` at build time with `scandelete`/`scanignore` allowlists; a maintainer states there is _"no official process for building a project from scratch using the existing tools for React Native"_ ([forum](https://forum.f-droid.org/t/how-to-submit-a-react-native-app-to-f-droid/9896)). At least one RN app is live ([EteSync Notes](https://f-droid.org/en/packages/com.etesync.notes/)), with recurring build breakage documented ([3398](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroiddata/-/issues/3398), [2648 — react-native-reanimated](https://gitlab.com/fdroid/fdroiddata/-/issues/2648)).

### Awesome lists — free, PR-based, no legal entity

- **[dreamingechoes/awesome-mental-health](https://github.com/dreamingechoes/awesome-mental-health)** — 3.6k stars, has an Applications section (Calm, Headspace, Wysa, Woebot, Quirk). ☠️ **Its clinical-evidence clause explicitly exempts small apps**: _"it's important that there is high-quality clinical evidence for it. Small tools or apps, and articles of personal experience are exempt from this"_ ([contributing](https://github.com/dreamingechoes/awesome-mental-health/blob/master/.github/CONTRIBUTING.md)). **Last push 2025-05-02 — no 2026 commits.** The marketing plan's ruling that PR #90 is a dead letter stands on _abandonment_; if anyone ever re-argues it on the evidence bar, that argument is wrong.
- **[theimpossibleastronaut/awesome-mentalhealth](https://github.com/theimpossibleastronaut/awesome-mentalhealth)** — _"anything remotely related to mental health"_, has an Apps section, **no clinical-evidence bar**, English required. **Last push 2026-03-31 — this one is alive**, and the marketing plan does not know it exists.
- **awesome-selfhosted** — ⚑ structurally excludes this app: software that is _"a desktop, **mobile**, or command-line application, which relies on a separate file synchronisation/server program"_ does not qualify, and a tagged release older than four months is required ([contributing](https://github.com/awesome-selfhosted/awesome-selfhosted-data/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md)). Very active.
- **awesome-digital-health** — no canonical repo found. The nearest active list, kakoni/awesome-healthcare (last push 2026-05-05), is scoped to EHR and hospital infrastructure with no consumer self-help category.
