# Search operations - audit health, a standings reading, and a read-only baseline for selftend.org

**Status:** Decided spec, assembled 2026-09-15 from wayfinder map [#2419](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2419) on its last ticket, [#2426](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2426). Every section links the ticket whose resolution comment holds the full reasoning and the owner's ruling. **Applied so far:** § 9 item 1, the Ahrefs project settings, on [#2461](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2461) (2026-09-15, Health Score 88); and § 9 items 3 and 4, the doc amendments and the two glossary terms, in the same change as this file (§ 8). § 9 item 2, the two privacy-page links - the only code this spec asks for and the only thing between 88 and 100 - built on [#2476](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2476) (2026-09-16); the next scheduled audit reads the result.
**Audience:** Whoever reads the monthly Ahrefs audit on the 9th and whoever sits the 2026-12-10 reading; developers for § 9 item 2.
**The word:** the site is kept **readable** and never **pursued** ([marketing-plan.md](marketing-plan.md) § 4, square 3, from [#2289](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2289)). This spec adds no copy, no page and no link chosen for a query. It decides three things and nothing more: what the audit is allowed to hide and what it must show, whether and how the site's search standing is read, and what the December call compares against. Every number here is a reading, never an arrival and never a channel judgement (§ 3 of the plan).

## 3. The health-score policy - fix, exclude, turn off, accept

[#2424](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2424), twelve rulings. Mechanics from [#2421](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2421): the Health Score counts **Error-severity rows only, per URL**; warnings and notices never move it. The only levers that leave the site untouched are per-**issue** turn-off or demotion and crawl **exclusion** by URL pattern - none is per URL, and a turn-off hides that issue on every URL forever. Free Webmaster Tools keeps every lever for the verified site; exports are 0 rows on Free and Starter alike. Google uses no third-party score: the number is Ahrefs' own consistency check, read as one.

| #   | Finding                                        | Ruling                                                                               | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1-2 | `404 page`, `4XX page` on the two auth URLs    | **Exclude** the two URLs from the crawl by pattern                                   | The only lever scoped to exactly those URLs. A per-issue turn-off would hide every future 404 on the site - the regression the audit exists to catch. Their 404 is verified by [deployment.md](deployment.md)'s post-deploy check instead                                                |
| 3   | `Orphan page` ×2                               | **Fix on its own terms**; the issue **stays on**                                     | A navigation rendered as a button is not a link. `/security` and `/account-deletion` are linked from `/privacy` with strings that already exist (§ 9 item 2). An orphan is a true fact a reader should see; an unexplained one is "investigate", never "regression"                      |
| 4   | `Page has links to broken page` ×1             | **Cleared by the exclusion** (observed, § 4)                                         | Never a turn-off: that would hide every future broken internal link                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| 5   | `3XX redirect` ×1 (`www`)                      | **Accept**, visible; target mode stays Subdomains                                    | The row is § 6.1 working. A 3XX on an apex route would be a real regression the same issue reports. Narrowing the scope gains nothing: the score is 100 with `www` in the denominator                                                                                                    |
| 6-7 | `Meta description too short` ×5, `too long` ×1 | **Turn off** for the project                                                         | The subline-verbatim rule makes a length warning never actionable, so a future one is never a regression either. Truncation and duplication surface under different issues, which stay on                                                                                                |
| 8-9 | The two AI-bot notices ×8 each                 | **Turn off**                                                                         | The rows move only when Ahrefs or Cloudflare edits a list, never when the site does. The policy is stated once, in indexability § 6.5 with its deployment.md row. `public/robots.txt` does not mirror the block at origin (Cloudflare prepends it; mirroring would duplicate every line) |
| 10  | `only one dofollow incoming internal link` ×5  | **Accept**, on, expected with its count                                              | A true reading a product decision may use one day; a count change is worth seeing. Cross-linking is a product-surface question ([brand-result.md](brand-result.md) § 7 owns the footer)                                                                                                  |
| 11  | `Indexable page not in sitemap` ×8             | **Fix in project settings**: tick Auto-detected sitemaps, untick the custom URL list | Pure artefact. Auto-detected reads the `Sitemap:` line in `robots.txt`, so it verifies that line too. The custom list was a second copy of the index list held in Ahrefs, which drifts - a route removed later would be crawled as a 404 and read as a false regression                  |

**What each lever now fails to catch, stated.** The exclusion: the audit no longer verifies that the two auth URLs answer 404 - deployment.md's post-deploy check does. The four turn-offs: a future description outside Ahrefs' length band, and any change in the AI-bot comparison; neither can be a regression under the spec. Nothing else is hidden: 404s, broken links, redirects, orphans, canonicals, duplicates and sitemap membership all stay on.

**The record of the policy is [#2424](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2424) and this section.** Ahrefs' "Turned off" tab is the mechanism, never the record. Never re-enable a turned-off issue or "fix" a by-design 404 to move the score.

## 5. The monthly audit as it is read

The paragraph below is the runbook's ([operations-runbook.md](operations-runbook.md) § _Recurring Checks_), applied in this change; it is repeated here because this file owns the reasons. The audit is a build-health reading that is nobody's marketing duty.

> **Monthly, on the 9th - the Ahrefs Site Audit of `selftend.org`** (project 10345616; Execute JavaScript on; URL sources: the website plus the auto-detected sitemap, no custom list; the two `(auth)` URLs excluded from the crawl by pattern; four issues turned off for the project - the two description-length warnings and the two AI-bot notices - because none of them can move when the site does). It reads whether the site is still readable to a crawler: every page on the index list crawled with its own title, description and canonical, no duplicate group, the Health Score at **100** over 16 crawled URLs (8 pages, `sitemap.xml`, the `www` 301, 3 resources, 3 `robots.txt` fetches) - 88 until the two privacy-page links ship. **Expected rows, never a regression:** `3XX redirect` ×1 (the `www` seed answering § 6.1's redirect) and `only one dofollow incoming internal link` ×7 (×5, plus `Orphan page` ×2, until those links ship). **Noise, not a regression:** `Slow page` on a single crawl, and `Pages added to sitemaps` on the crawl after a sitemap change. **An audit regression:** any Error on an index-list URL, any issue type not named here, a change in a listed page's status, canonical or description, or `Slow page` on the same page two audits running. A regression is a build defect and goes to a GitHub issue, never to the marketing plan - the plan reads Search Console once a year, on 2026-12-10, coverage and one standings reading beside it, and nothing else. Google's emails on indexing issues, manual actions and security are default-on and are the coverage alarm between sittings; do not unsubscribe. The `(auth)` routes' 404 is verified by deployment.md's post-deploy check, not by the audit. Never re-enable a turned-off issue or "fix" a by-design 404 to move the score. If the turned-off list or the exclusion is missing after the Ahrefs plan change, re-apply it from [#2424](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2424) before reading the crawl.

## 6. The standings reading - once a year, and what it may trigger

[#2425](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2425), eight decisions. Instruments from [#2422](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2422): Search Console is the only instrument reading Google's side and is complete on the free tier (16 months of per-query data in the UI, two to three days behind); its coverage, manual-action and security emails are default-on, and **no performance digest exists anywhere**, so any impressions or queries figure is a login. A named query row is an **anonymisation-threshold crossing** - a few dozen distinct searchers over two to three months - never a first search. Ahrefs Free keeps the verified site's referring-domain count and organic-keywords snapshot, with no history and no exports. The one reading no free instrument gives is position history for a keyword list the owner types, and typing that list is a choice made _for a query_ - where pursuit begins.

**The decision.** The site's search standing is read **once a year, on 2026-12-10, beside the coverage read the sitting already carries - and nowhere else.** No monthly and no quarterly clock: at 22 impressions a month a number read more often is noise, § 7 of the plan says the sitting "creates no monthly duty", and nothing may act on a standings figure before the call anyway. The plan's "and nothing else" sentence is amended to say so (§ 8), not fitted under.

**The instrument, at $0:** Search Console's Performance and Page indexing reports, with URL Inspection per route when a dated truth is needed; Ahrefs Free's snapshot; Google's default-on emails. **No paid tier. Bing Webmaster Tools has no reading duty** - indexability § 9 stands as written: importing the property is an owner step, its clock starts at import, and a Bing line would add a sign-in to every sitting for a second engine's copy of the same zero.

**What the sitting reads:** Search Console impressions and clicks, last 28 days and the property's life, set against the previous reading's file; the named query rows over the property's life with **first occurrences marked**; Ahrefs Free's referring-domain count and organic-keywords snapshot. **Not read:** position, CTR, device, country, weekly charts, backlink counts, anything per page - position and CTR on 22 impressions carry nothing, and a per-page reading would be a verdict on a page, which [#2399](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2399) refused.

**Where it is recorded:** a dated, never-edited file per reading on a throwaway `research/search-standings-<date>` branch, never merged, the same shape as the baseline (§ 7), naming the file it compares against and the sign-in state and locale beside any SERP figure. The runbook carries no figures.

**What "Search Console shows Bulgarian queries reaching the site" means.** The plan's second reopen finding had zero rows to read. It now means: **a named query row in the 16-month view whose query text is Bulgarian, at any impression count, read at the sitting.** The threshold is the meaning. Bulgaria-_country_ impressions do not count: they sit almost entirely on `/`, are mostly brand and owner searches, and prove nothing about subject demand. Because no query email exists, a row appearing in October waits for December.

**What stands in for "read in Ahrefs' index on the day".** Keywords Explorer is gone after 2026-10-10, so the sizing half of square 3's trigger cannot be re-read on the day. **The 2026-09-15 baseline's figures stand as the read.** They moved about 5% between 10 Sep and 15 Sep, and a December re-read changes no decision the day makes. A fresh Starter month ($29; a fresh subscription, never a lapsed one, [costs.md](costs.md)) is bought only **after** the call promotes a pond search serves and that pond needs fresh sizing - never before the call, and never by a reading.

## Appendix A - premises corrected while assembling

1. **"The custom URL list may still carry `www` twins, inflating the denominator."** Charting's guess. The list held exactly the eight apex URLs; the `www` 301 is the crawler's own seed under Subdomains mode ([#2420](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2420)).
2. **"The 11 Sep crawl is stale - it ran hours after the redirect went live."** A fresh crawl gave identical per-issue counts ([#2420](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2420)).
3. **"44 issues = 7 errors, 7 warnings, 30 notices, across ten types."** 43 across eleven: charting's list missed `only one dofollow incoming internal link` ×5, and the sums only close with it ([#2420](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2420)).
4. **"The eight 'not in sitemap' notices mean the crawl's URL set is not the index list."** The sitemap was never fetched: both sitemap URL sources were unticked. The URL set _was_ the index list ([#2420](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2420)).
5. **"The orphans might be a seed effect of the custom list."** They are orphans on the live site: no public `href` reaches either. And dropping the custom list did not lose them - the sitemap now seeds them ([#2420](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2420), [#2461](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2461)).
6. **"`Page has links to broken page` may survive the exclusion, so the score may be 94."** It does not survive: the targets read `Not crawled` / `Not matching include rules` and cannot be judged broken. The `94` variant is dead ([#2461](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2461)).
7. **"The Health Score denominator is 17", then "12", then "13".** It is **16** = the HTTP-status distribution; the Overview's 12 and the crawl log's 13 are different counts of the same crawl ([#2461](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2461)).
8. **"The one-inlink notice reads ×7 after the settings."** ×5 until § 9 item 2 ships; ×7 only after, because the count assumed the links ([#2461](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2461)).
9. **"The URL sources are the website plus the custom list of the eight routes"** (the runbook and indexability § 9 as written on 2026-09-10). Website plus the auto-detected sitemap, no custom list, since 2026-09-15 ([#2461](https://github.com/Selftend/selftend/issues/2461)).
