That means:

- product-flow ideas are allowed
- contributor-process ideas are allowed
- code copy is not allowed without explicit review
- text/content/asset copy is not allowed without explicit review

## Books

The module specs are written from books, under the same rule: structure and skill names may be paraphrased, text may not be copied. Each spec cites its own source in its header; the two below are recorded here because their titles and worksheets are copyrighted material a contributor may be tempted to lift.

### McKay, Wood & Brantley - the DBT skills workbook (2nd ed., New Harbinger, 2019, ISBN 978-1-68403-458-1)

Role in this repo:

- the source of the DBT module spec (`docs/modules/dbt-mckay-skills-workbook.md`): its four skill groups, their order, and the shape of its exercises
- skill names used as terms of art; every exercise paraphrased and classified build / link / learn-only / omit

Current reuse status:

- no text, checklist, worksheet or vignette copied
- the authors' own mnemonics and worksheet titles are not lifted
- ⚠️ the published title spells the therapy's name in American English and is never written out in the repository (`test/positioning-copy.test.ts`)

### Linehan - DBT Skills Training Manual (2nd ed., Guilford, 2015, ISBN 978-1-4625-1699-5)

Role in this repo:

- the public DBT vocabulary only: the four skill-group names, _wise mind_, _radical acceptance_, _opposite action_, and the acronyms the DBT learn pages name beside their expansion (STOP, PLEASE, DEAR MAN, FAST)

Current reuse status:

- no content taken; no handout or worksheet text copied
- the "DBT®" mark is an open question for the pre-launch legal review (`docs/licensing.md` § _Important caveat_)
