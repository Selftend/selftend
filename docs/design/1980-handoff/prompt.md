## What you are designing

You are designing **six screens of a new module** inside **Selftend**, a free, non-profit, open-source mental-health app. The module is **DBT — Dialectical behaviour therapy**. It has a home, a coping plan, one timed session, five kinds of dated record, a programme card and a set of learn pages; you draw **six** of those surfaces and leave the rest to shipped patterns (the appendix says which pattern, so nothing is invented). The copy is decided and is given to you verbatim. Your job is layout, hierarchy and states, on the app's existing visual system and shared components, in a way one or two people can build in React Native. Do not rewrite the copy. Where a line seems wrong, say so in a note rather than changing it.

Selftend runs on iOS, Android and the web (a responsive web app, also installable as a PWA). Every screen ships in English and Bulgarian, in light and dark, across eight user-selectable colour palettes. Design in tokens, not hexes, and leave visible room for Bulgarian, which usually runs longer. One Bulgarian noun is already decided and is a fair length sample: **Distress tolerance** is **Устойчивост на стрес**.

### What Selftend is — for orientation only

> **Selftend is a set of free, private mental health tools:** everyday tools for right now, and a **CBT programme** — cognitive behavioural therapy — **to work through when you want one.**

That sentence belongs to the surfaces a person meets _before_ they are inside the product. **Every DBT screen is past that threshold, so none of them carries it.** The frame of every DBT surface is the module's own sentence:

> **Skills for when feelings run high - for getting through the moment without making it worse, and for changing what keeps bringing it back.**

Vocabulary: **DBT** is the module; _Dialectical behaviour therapy_ is spelled out in the h1, **DBT** after. **Programme** means the module's four-phase progression and is never the category. A **skill group** is one of the module's four sections, and also one of the programme's four phases. A **record** is a saved wise mind check-in, judgement, emotion record, opposite-action plan or script. A **session** is a completed timed practice. The **coping plan** is one per person and is neither a record nor a session. Never **DBT®**.

### Who it is for, and the state they arrive in

There is no target segment, and this brief must not invent one. What is decided is a **use context**: _for when feelings run high_. Do not turn it into a trait, a condition or a diagnosis — not "people with intense emotions", not "people with BPD", not any group. Two of the six surfaces (the coping-plan card, and the Stop on the session) are opened _in_ a hard moment: design them to be read in one glance, with nothing to decide before the plan or the exit is visible.

Selftend is for people aged 13 and over (or their country's higher floor). There is no separate teen mode; protections are the same for everyone. Nothing reads adults-only, and nothing asks about the person beyond the surface's own fields.

## ☠️ The three hard rules — the instructions most likely to be lost

Each has a reading test. Answer all three in a note on every screen.

> **1. Nothing on a DBT surface changes because of what the person entered.** No rating-triggered prompt, no "are you safe?", no interstitial that appears because of a word or a score. The crisis affordance is **always on and never triggered**: the red `CrisisSupportCallout` card on the module home; the hairline `CrisisSupportBar` on every entry screen (every `/new`, the builder, every flow step, every session intro and run screen, the learn pages). The one line that names danger is a **static step**, identical for everyone (Pause and choose's first beat).
> _Reading test: does anything on this screen appear, hide or change because of the person's input? The answer must be no._

> **2. Stop saves nothing and asks nothing.** Every timed session and every multi-step flow has a plain, always-visible **Stop** that ends it at once, records nothing, returns to where the person came from and says nothing about it — no "are you sure", no "you were close", no partial record. The back gesture _is_ Stop. ☠️ This is the **opposite** of the shipped tools' _Finish early_ (which saves a partial row) and their back-gesture finish-or-continue dialog; draw it as a departure and label it on the screen.
> _Reading test: is a way out visible without scrolling, and does taking it leave a trace? Visible; none._

> **3. Fulfilling, and done.** Every save ends on a line that states the record and stops. No "come back", no age on an open plan or script, no counts of anything caught or done, no overdue mark, no streak, no recent-records feed. A read surface opened in a hard moment (the coping-plan card) shows the plan and **no warning above it** — it is the one entry surface with no crisis bar.
> _Reading test: does anything on this screen ask to be reopened, or vary by date or visit? No._

Also binding, stated once: the word **crisis** means only what the crisis page means; **emergency** is never a label; a **caution** is two sentences of plain text above **Start**, never a modal, never acknowledged, never stored; module identity is icon and label, **never colour** — the skill-group badge is an ordinal, not an encoding.

## The six screens

Common to all six: the shared shell; the 720px content column for the home, the 620px column for forms and details; 44px touch targets; MaterialIcons through the app's `Icon` wrapper; headings semantic on the web (one h1 per screen). A field's _hint_ is the copy under its label. Every string below is verbatim from the spec.

### Screen 2 — the coping plan: builder and card (`/modules/dbt/coping-plan`, `/edit`)

One plan per person, **one document** (items plus an ordered fallback list). There is no per-item entity, so there is no per-item detail, swipe or menu. Two surfaces of one record.

**The builder** (`/edit`, and the intro at `/coping-plan` when no plan exists) — a form on `MobileFormScreen` with `ScreenTopBar` (`close`), the **`CrisisSupportBar`** at the top, three `Section`s and the list:

| Section                         | What it holds                                                                                                                                                                       | Copy you work with                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Distract**                    | six pick families, about six picks each, as chip runs under a family label; an _Add your own_ line per section (free text, up to 120 characters)                                    | Families: **Move** · **Make or fix something** · **Someone else** · **Change the channel** · **Count something** · **Leave**. Example picks (the spec finalises the set): _walk_, _stretch_, _shower_, _dance to one song_; _tidy one surface_, _water the plants_; _message a friend_, _look at a photo of someone you care about_; _a show_, _a song_, _a place in your head_; _breaths_, _backwards from 100 by sevens_; _step outside_, _another room_ |
| **Soothe**                      | five families, one per sense, about six picks each                                                                                                                                  | **Smell** · **See** · **Hear** · **Taste** · **Touch**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Remind myself**               | about eight app-written lines plus own lines — this section's real content is the person's own words                                                                                | _this will pass_ · _I have got through hard moments before_ · _feeling it is not the same as acting on it_ · _mistakes are normal_ · _feelings are a wave_                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **If that doesn't work, next…** | the **fallback list**: 3–6 items chosen from the plan's own picks and lines, **reordered by drag** (the app's sortable-grid pattern), each with an optional **at home only** toggle | Save requires 3–6; removing an item from a section removes it from the list and the list re-numbers                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |

Some picks **carry a route** and render as tappable on the card: _a paced breathing session_ → Breathing · _5-4-3-2-1_ → Grounding · _cool water on my wrists_ → Grounding's cold-water technique · _tense and release_ → the muscle-relaxation session · _plan something I enjoy_ → Activities · _an ambient sound_ → Meditation · _write it down_ → Journal. The builder marks them with a small glyph. Footer: the shipped `Save` (`Discard` only on an edit of an existing plan). **Save lands on the card** with one line, _Your plan is ready_, and stops.

**The card** (`/coping-plan` when a plan exists) — the read surface for a hard moment. `ScreenTopBar` (`back`); **no `CrisisSupportBar`** (the ruled exception); **no completion affordance, no "I used this", no last-used, no count**. Order: the **fallback list first, in large type**, numbered as a sequence — _First…_ · _If that doesn't help…_ — with the _at home only_ mark as a small glyph on its item; then the Distract and Soothe picks as compact rows (route-bearing ones tappable, opening the tool); then the Remind-myself lines as quiet text; a small **Edit** last. Opening the card writes nothing. **Offline**: on iOS and Android the card works without a connection for a day after the module was last opened; on the web it needs a connection. Draw no offline badge and no offline copy — the only offline state is the shipped error card with retry when nothing is cached.

**States**: builder (a) empty intro — bar, one paragraph of what the plan is, the three empty sections, the list needing three; (b) in progress with picks, own lines and a partial list (save disabled with the shipped inline validation, _Choose three to six for the list_); (c) editing an existing plan; card (d) populated with a 4-item list, one home-only mark, three tappable picks; (e) the post-save line; (f) the shipped error card with retry. **Coverage**: 360 light for the builder; 360 light **and dark** for the card. **At 360**: the numbered fallback items in large type must survive a two-line item; chips wrap; the drag handle stays a 44px target. **Departures**: a read surface with no bar; a document edited whole; picks that are doors. **Not to design**: the pick copy (the spec's), a per-item screen, any completion or history.

### Screen 4 — the timed session: muscle relaxation (`/modules/dbt/sessions/muscle-relaxation`)

Three screens of one route: intro → run → done. Inherits the breathing session's timed engine (before-shots `S4`: the _ready_ screen, then the running countdown in `FocusSessionShell`) and the grounding flow's step shape (Back/Next, a segment bar) — and **departs from both on exit**.

**Intro**: crumb, h1 **Muscle relaxation**, one line of what it is (_Tense and release, one muscle group at a time._), the **caution** as `TechniqueCaution` (two lines, always visible, never acknowledged): **_Tense gently, never to the point of pain, and let go quickly. Skip any area that hurts — back, neck, joints._** / **_If you are pregnant or faint easily, keep it light or leave it._**; the **variant** as a `SegmentedControl` — **Full** (_about 12 muscle groups, 12–15 minutes_) · **Short** (_five combined poses, about 5 minutes_); **Start**; the **`CrisisSupportBar`**.

**Run**: `FocusSessionShell` with eyebrow _Muscle relaxation_ and trailing `Group 3 of 12 · 9:40 left`; `ProgressSegments` in step mode; the muscle group as the heading; the instruction beneath (_tense… for five seconds_ → _and let go_ for twenty, twice per group, auto-advancing on the countdown; the copy says _gently_ only); the countdown numeral; controls **Back · Next** and **Pause** (kept from the breathing engine — a paused clock saves nothing either), and **Stop** — plain, always visible, no dialog, saves nothing, returns to the module home. The back gesture is Stop. The bar renders on the run screen too. Reduced motion respected: no shot relies on animation.

**Done** (completion only): states the record — **_Muscle relaxation, 14 minutes_** — one **Done** button back to the module home. Nothing else: no rating, no "how do you feel", no "come back tomorrow". The once-ever reminder offer is the shipped post-completion card and is **not** drawn.

**States**: (a) intro, Full selected; (b) running, mid-group, tense beat; (c) running, release beat; (d) paused; (e) done. **Coverage**: 360 light **and dark** (the run screen is the night surface). **At 360**: the countdown numeral, the heading and the four controls without the controls leaving the fold. **Departures — draw and label them**: **Stop** replaces _Finish early_ (no partial row; the shipped tools save one); back gesture = Stop (the shipped tools open a finish-or-continue dialog); a caution above Start; record on completion only. **Not to design**: the reminder offer card, a sessions history (there is none in the first slice), a pause-resume dialog.

### Screen 5 — the script: builder step 2 and the card (`/modules/dbt/scripts/new`, `/[id]`)

Two surfaces of one record. **The builder** is a three-step `WizardScreen` with the persisted draft and the shipped discard dialog, **`CrisisSupportBar` on `/new` only**. You draw **step 2**; steps 1 and 3 follow the shipped wizard and are described:

- **Step 1 · What's going on** — `situation` (text, required; hint _The problem, in a line or two — what keeps happening._) and **What would you want changed?** as four chips: _More of_ · _Less of_ · _Stop_ · _Start_ (optional).
- **Step 2 · The script** (drawn) — four lines in order, each with its hint visible:

| Line                        | Field                                                                                                      | Hint                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **I think**                 | text, required                                                                                             | _Just the facts — what happened, not why you think they did it._                                                                                |
| **I feel**                  | an emotion from the check-in's list (`PickerSheet`, customs inline), optional, plus a short line, optional | _Your feeling, in your own words. "I feel hurt", never "I feel that you…" — that is a thought about them wearing a feeling's clothes._          |
| **I want**                  | text, required                                                                                             | _One thing, specific, something they could do this week. Not "be nicer" — "text me if you'll be late."_                                         |
| **What I'll do for myself** | text, optional                                                                                             | _If the answer is no: what you will do for yourself. Something you do for you, not something you do to them — and you can keep it to yourself._ |

- **Step 3 · Before the conversation** — **How hard does this one feel?** as `NumberRating` 0–100 in steps of ten (optional; read by the list order only); **When and where** (free text, optional; hint _a calm moment, not mid-argument_); one hint line, storing nothing: _How hard to push? Ask yourself how much you need this, and how much the relationship can take right now._

**The card** (`/[id]`) — the reopen-before-the-conversation surface, **no bar**. `ScreenTopBar` (`back`); the **four lines large, in order** (I think · I feel · I want · What I'll do for myself — the last shown quietly, it is the person's reserve); beneath them a `Disclosure` labelled **If they push back**, collapsed, holding five one-line skills read-only: _grant the true part_ · _say the one sentence again, calmly_ · _ask what exactly bothers them_ · _agree with what is true, let the always and never go_ · _ask for time_; then **Done** — opens one optional sheet, **How did it go?** (text), and closes the script with its done-day. Done copy: _Done: <the I want line>_, and stops. Delete from the card (the shipped destructive row); **no edit route**. No age on an open script, nothing waiting.

**States**: builder (a) step 2 empty with hints; (b) step 2 with the emotion picked and three lines typed; card (c) open, disclosure collapsed; (d) disclosure expanded; (e) the _How did it go?_ sheet; (f) done — the done-day and the note shown, Done gone. **Coverage**: 360 light. **At 360**: four large lines where _I want_ may run to three lines; the disclosure trigger a 44px row. **Departures**: a wizard whose second step is the whole point (the shipped wizards spread evenly); a card with no bar; Done from a read surface (Activities' shape). **Not to design**: steps 1 and 3 beyond the description, the list (appendix: the ladder), the discard dialog, the picker internals.

- **(d) Dismissed after graduation**: the shipped **Replay** row — _Replay the DBT programme_.
- **Absent**: dismissed invitation, nothing started — block 2 renders nothing; the header flag restores it.

**Coverage**: 360 light and dark (it sits on the home). **At 360**: two-line milestone labels against the `current/target` column. **Departures**: none in component; the departures are the filtered graduation (a match to CBT, a departure from ACT) and the crisis card staying beside the programme card unchanged by phase. **Not to design**: the card's internals beyond the states (it is a shipped component), the reminder, any Home or launcher surface (there is none for DBT).

## Product rules that shape layout

- **Free, non-profit, no ads, no subscriptions, no paywall.** Nothing implies a tier, a trial or an upsell.
- **Optional everything.** Streaks do not exist. The one DBT reminder is opt-in, off by default, offered once ever, and never mentions a missed day.
- **Fulfilling, and done.** A surface is used and left, not reopened. No engagement mechanics, no feed, no counts of things done.
- **Modular.** The module is reached from Modules and Home favourites; nothing preselects it.
- **AI is not in the product.** No assistant, no chat, no generated content.
- **Crisis guidance is visible, calm and clearly separate.** The callout and the bar are shipped components; their text is not yours.
- **The person is the only reader of their records.** No score, no pattern, no suggestion computed from what they wrote.
