Favourites, twice over

Home renders Favourites, then all eight tools, then all three modules — so a favourited item appears twice on one screen, by design. Today the two appearances agree, because Favourites is the catalogue filtered. Starred-at order breaks that agreement. The question is whether the disagreement reads as broken — so look at the screen before you turn the numbering on.
