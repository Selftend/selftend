What this bench is for

  • ☠ The dashed line is the scroll cap, not a decoration: menuMaxHeight = round(windowHeight * 0.7) (user-menu.tsx:80). Anything in the shaded zone needs a scroll to reach. The failure #1774 fixed was exactly “the actions became unreachable”.
  • The action row is flex-row flex-wrap gap-3 and every Button carries shrink-0, so buttons never compress — they wrap. Sign out’s grow basis-auto is what makes the third one take its own full-width line instead of being crushed to ~32 px, which is the bug the code comments.
  • The identity row shows what #1810 decided: Guest / Гост with a person glyph, no sub-line, and the row itself is not pressable — so the door needs its own control wherever it lands.
  • “Saved a name” is not a curiosity: Edit name has no guest guard, so a guest can hold a real name and lose the word Guest while still being a guest. Shape B has to survive a long name beside its button.
  • Labels here are provisional — Sign in / Влез, borrowed from auth:signUp.signInLink. Final copy is the spec’s call. ☠ “Switch account” is forbidden by CONTEXT.md §Abandonment.
